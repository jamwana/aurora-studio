韶光 AURORA，源于对东方美学的深沉敬意。我们相信，真正的美不在于繁复的装饰，而在于留白中的气韵、简约中的深意。每一尊人偶，都是对千年东方文明的一次当代诠释——以手工艺的温度，凝固时光中那一抹不可复制的惊鸿。 AURORA was born from a profound reverence for Oriental aesthetics. We believe true beauty lies not in ornate decoration, but in the spirit within emptiness, the depth within simplicity. Each doll is a contemporary interpretation of millennia of Eastern civilization — a breath of irreplicable beauty, frozen in time through the warmth of artisanal craft.

金衣云裳 GILDED CLOUD 裁一段日光，洇入流金 Cutting a ray of sunlight, bleeding into flowing gold
"裁一段日光，洇入流金。" "A ray of sunlight, dissolved into liquid gold."

系列的首章，亦是关于"光"的初次探索。重磅真丝哑金面料，配合极致精细的苏绣工法，试图捕捉晚霞掠过宫檐的那一抹瞬时。 The opening chapter — an exploration of "light." Heavyweight matte-gold silk, paired with supremely refined Suzhou embroidery, capturing the fleeting moment when dusk sweeps across palace eaves.

SILK EMBROIDERY PHOENIX PEARL
墨韵帝华 INK EMPEROR 心有赤子，静水深流 A pure heart, deep waters running still
"心有赤子，静水深流。" "With a pure heart, the deepest waters flow in silence."

红色在东方语境中从不只是色彩，而是厚重的生命力。此章聚焦于庄重与温婉的平衡，珠翠交辉间，黑红金三色构建出权力场域的无声叙事。 Red in the Oriental context is never merely a color — it is the weight of vitality. Black, red, and gold construct a silent narrative of power.

竹韵清歌 BAMBOO SONG 虚心有节，清风自来 Hollow yet resilient, the clear breeze comes unbidden
"虚心有节，清风自来。" "Hollow in heart, firm in character."

竹，东方文人的精神图腾。此章以翠绿色为主调，金线绣竹叶点缀其间，黑狐绒肩饰增添华贵层次。 Bamboo — the spiritual totem of Oriental literati. Emerald green with gold-embroidered leaves, black fur accents.

BAMBOO EMERALD GOLD THREAD
红罗雅韵 SCARLET VERSE 牡丹亭畔，惊梦一瞬 Beside the Peony Pavilion, a moment of awakening
"牡丹亭畔，惊梦一瞬。" "Beside the Peony Pavilion — a dream awakened."

正红为底，牡丹为魂。立体高浮雕苏绣牡丹纹样在绸缎上绽放，蓝紫白三色丝线交织出盛唐气象。 True red as foundation, peony as soul. Three-dimensional Suzhou embroidery peonies bloom across silk.

PEONY SCARLET TANG DYNASTY
幻羽流光 ETHEREAL JADE 雾失楼台，月逐流波 Towers lost in mist, moonlight chasing waves
"雾失楼台，月逐流波。" "Towers dissolve in mist, moonlight follows the waves."

挑战透明度的极限。轻如蝉翼的特制绢纱，通过层叠覆盖营造出烟雨朦胧的意境。 Pushing the limits of translucency. Specially crafted silk gauze, lighter than a cicada's wing, layered to create ethereal mood.

秋金 AUTUMN GOLD 云肩叠翠，蝶舞金秋 Layered cloud collar, butterflies in autumn gold
"云肩叠翠，蝶舞金秋。" "Layered cloud shoulders, butterflies dancing in autumn gold."

芥黄金色缎袍搭配紫色云肩，蝴蝶纹样居中，珍珠边框镶饰。此章是对"丰盛"的礼赞。 A mustard-gold satin robe paired with a purple cloud collar, butterfly motif at center. A celebration of abundance.

苏绣 Suzhou Embroidery 非遗工艺 Intangible Heritage

每一片花瓣、每一只蝴蝶，皆由苏绣匠人以手工一针一线绣制。丝线细如发丝，色彩过渡自然如水墨。 Every petal, every butterfly — hand-stitched by Suzhou masters. Silk threads finer than hair.
