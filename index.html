东方美学 Oriental Aesthetics

以器载道 Crafted with Soul 东方美学的当代收藏 Contemporary Oriental Aesthetics

韶光 AURORA，源于对东方美学的深沉敬意。我们相信，真正的美不在于繁复的装饰，而在于留白中的气韵、简约中的深意。每一尊人偶，都是对千年东方文明的一次当代诠释——以手工艺的温度，凝固时光中那一抹不可复制的惊鸿。 AURORA was born from a profound reverence for Oriental aesthetics. We believe true beauty lies not in ornate decoration, but in the spirit within emptiness, the depth within simplicity. Each doll is a contemporary interpretation of millennia of Eastern civilization — a breath of irreplicable beauty, frozen in time through the warmth of artisanal craft.

Chapter 01

金衣云裳 Gilded Cloud 裁一段日光，洇入流金 Cutting a ray of sunlight, bleeding into flowing gold

"裁一段日光，洇入流金。" "A ray of sunlight, dissolved into liquid gold."

系列的首章，亦是关于"光"的初次探索。重磅真丝哑金面料，配合极致精细的苏绣工法，试图捕捉晚霞掠过宫檐的那一抹瞬时。它不只是玩偶的衣裳，而是一个关于权力和浪漫的古老梦境。凤凰金饰、珍珠垂坠、白狐绒领，每一处细节皆是权力与柔情的交织。 The opening chapter — an exploration of "light." Heavyweight matte-gold silk, paired with supremely refined Suzhou embroidery, attempts to capture the fleeting moment when dusk sweeps across palace eaves. More than a doll's attire, it is an ancient dream of power and romance. Phoenix gold ornaments, pearl tassels, white fur collar — every detail weaves authority and tenderness.

Chapter 02

墨韵帝华 Ink Emperor 心有赤子，静水深流 A pure heart, deep waters running still

"心有赤子，静水深流。" "With a pure heart, the deepest waters flow in silence."

红色在东方语境中从不只是色彩，而是厚重的生命力。此章聚焦于庄重与温婉的平衡，珠翠交辉间，黑红金三色构建出权力场域的无声叙事。珠帘半面，长辫垂背，手持团扇——一位不可侵犯的东方女皇，以静制动，以柔克刚。 Red in the Oriental context is never merely a color — it is the weight of vitality. This chapter balances solemnity and grace, where black, red, and gold construct a silent narrative of power. Half-veiled face, a long braid down the back, a round fan in hand — an inviolable Eastern empress, mastering stillness over motion, softness over force.

Chapter 03

竹韵清歌 Bamboo Song 虚心有节，清风自来 Hollow yet resilient, the clear breeze comes unbidden

"虚心有节，清风自来。" "Hollow in heart, firm in character — the clear wind arrives of its own accord."

竹，东方文人的精神图腾。此章以翠绿色为主调，金线绣竹叶点缀其间，黑狐绒肩饰增添华贵层次。绿色珠帘半遮面，仿若竹林深处的隐士，清逸脱俗，与世无争。金缕腰带垂落流苏，动静之间尽显东方文人的风骨与气节。 Bamboo — the spiritual totem of Oriental literati. Dominated by emerald green, gold-embroidered bamboo leaves scattered throughout, with black fur shoulder accents adding layers of luxury. A green beaded veil half-covers the face, like a hermit deep in a bamboo grove — refined, detached, at peace. The gold-thread belt with flowing tassels reveals the character and integrity of Oriental scholars in every gesture.

Chapter 04

红罗雅韵 Scarlet Verse 牡丹亭畔，惊梦一瞬 Beside the Peony Pavilion, a moment of awakening

"牡丹亭畔，惊梦一瞬。" "Beside the Peony Pavilion — a dream awakened in a single breath."

正红为底，牡丹为魂。立体高浮雕苏绣牡丹纹样在绸缎上绽放，蓝紫白三色丝线交织出盛唐气象。红石榴发饰与金蝶步摇呼应，白色广袖飘逸如云。这是关于"爱"与"梦"的章节——《牡丹亭》中杜丽娘的魂魄，跨越生死，只为那一眼惊鸿。 True red as the foundation, peony as the soul. Three-dimensional Suzhou embroidery peonies bloom across silk, where blue, purple, and white threads conjure the grandeur of the Tang dynasty. Red pomegranate hair ornaments echo golden butterfly pins; white wide sleeves float like clouds. This is a chapter about "love" and "dream" — the spirit of Du Liniang from The Peony Pavilion, crossing life and death, for that single breathtaking glance.

Chapter 05

幻羽流光 Ethereal Jade 雾失楼台，月逐流波 Towers lost in mist, moonlight chasing the flowing waves

"雾失楼台，月逐流波。" "Towers dissolve in mist, moonlight follows the rippling waves."

挑战透明度的极限。轻如蝉翼的特制绢纱，通过层叠覆盖营造出烟雨朦胧的意境。它是虚与实的边界，仿佛那尊灵动的人偶正处于从梦境迈入现实的刹那——轻盈、超脱、不可捉摸。珍珠白眼眸与银白发色，赋予她超越尘世的仙灵气质。 Pushing the limits of translucency. Specially crafted silk gauze, lighter than a cicada's wing, layered to create the ethereal mood of misty rain. It is the boundary between the void and the real — as if this agile doll stands at the very moment of stepping from dream into reality. Light, transcendent, elusive. Pearl-white eyes and silver-white hair grant her a fairy-like quality beyond the mortal realm.

Chapter 06

秋金 Autumn Gold 云肩叠翠，蝶舞金秋 Layered cloud collar, butterflies dancing in golden autumn

"云肩叠翠，蝶舞金秋。" "Layered cloud shoulders, butterflies dancing in autumn gold."

芥黄金色缎袍搭配紫色云肩（云肩），蝴蝶纹样居中，珍珠边框镶饰。高耸发髻上金色丝线与紫色花朵交映，珍珠簇拥间尽显宫廷贵气。此章是对"丰盛"的礼赞——秋收之金，华而不艳，雍容而内敛。 A mustard-gold satin robe paired with a purple cloud collar (yunjian), butterfly motif at center, pearl-bordered. The towering bun adorned with gold filigree and purple flowers, pearl clusters exuding palace elegance. This chapter celebrates "abundance" — the gold of autumn harvest, magnificent yet restrained, opulent yet understated.

高定工艺 Haute Couture Craft

匠心工坊 The Atelier 高定级别的纯手工制作 Haute Couture Craftsmanship

每一尊玩偶都是一段被凝固的时光。从妆面的精雕细琢到每一寸丝绸的严苛挑选，我们追求的不仅是外在的华丽，更是内在的风骨。Aurora 坚持"高定级别"的纯手工制作，让每一个细节都经得起审美的挑剔。 Each doll is a frozen moment of time — a breath suspended in resin and silk. From the meticulous carving of facial makeup to the rigorous selection of every inch of silk, we pursue not merely external splendor, but inner character. AURORA insists on haute couture-level handcraftsmanship, ensuring every detail withstands the most discerning aesthetic judgment.

绣

苏绣 Suzhou Embroidery 非遗工艺 Intangible Heritage Craft

每一片花瓣、每一只蝴蝶，皆由苏绣匠人以手工一针一线绣制。丝线细如发丝，色彩过渡自然如水墨。 Every petal, every butterfly — hand-stitched by Suzhou embroidery masters. Silk threads finer than hair, color transitions as natural as ink wash painting.

妆

手绘面妆 Hand-Painted Face 一笔一画皆灵魂 Every Stroke Has Soul

面妆由专业妆面师手工绘制，渐变唇色、眼影晕染、眉形勾勒，每一尊皆有独特的面部表情与灵魂。 Facial makeup hand-painted by professional artists — gradient lips, blended eyeshadow, defined brows. Each doll carries a unique expression and soul.

饰

金工珠宝 Goldsmith Jewelry 微观世界的精致 Refinement in Miniature

发簪、步摇、云肩、项链——每一件配饰皆按珠宝级别打造，珍珠、玉石、珊瑚、绿松石，镶嵌于金铜丝线之间。 Hairpins, buyao, cloud collars, necklaces — each accessory crafted at jewelry level. Pearls, jade, coral, turquoise, set between gold and copper wire.
